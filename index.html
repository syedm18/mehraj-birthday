A MESSAGE FROM THE HEART

✦ The Chapter I Was Never Meant to Own ✦

A letter to the girl who quietly became a part of my heart…

✦
With Love, From the Heart

Dear Mehraj,

I don't know what this letter will mean to you.

Maybe you'll read these words with a smile. Maybe with silence. Maybe you'll read everything and simply move on with your day.

And somehow, that's okay.

I'm not writing this to ask anything from you. I'm writing this because some feelings become too heavy to keep hidden forever.

So tonight, on your birthday, I wanted to leave a small piece of my heart here… just for you. 🤍

✦

🌙 SOME PEOPLE JUST ARRIVE…

Some people enter our lives like ordinary strangers.

No warning. No grand entrance. No promise that they'll stay.

And yet, somehow, they become a part of your thoughts, your memories, your prayers… and eventually,

a part of your heart.

For me, you became that person.

I never planned to fall for you. I never decided that you would become so important to me.

It simply happened. Slowly. Silently.

"You were already somewhere inside my heart before I even realized it."
✦

🥀 I TRIED TO FORGET YOU

I've tried to move on. I've told myself to stop thinking about you, to stop remembering the little things, and to accept that maybe I'm simply not the person you want.

But every time I told my heart to forget you…

somehow, it found another reason to remember you.

A message. A conversation. A smile. A look.

A moment that may have meant nothing to you, but somehow stayed with me.

"They don't have to do anything extraordinary to become extraordinary to you."
✦

🖤 AFTER YOUR NO…

You already gave me your answer.

I heard it. I didn't like it, but I understood it.

And somewhere inside me, I knew I had to respect it.

The difficult part was never understanding your answer. The difficult part was understanding why my heart didn't learn to let go at the same speed.

You said no… and somehow, my feelings still remained.

Not because I want to change your mind. Not because I want a place in your heart. And not because you owe me anything.

I simply couldn't pretend that you suddenly meant nothing to me.

"Sometimes the heart understands the truth long before it learns how to live with it."
✦

🌧️ MAYBE THIS IS WHAT ONE-SIDED LOVE IS

Loving someone without possessing them.

Wishing them happiness even when you aren't the reason for that happiness.

Caring without demanding. Remembering without expecting.

And quietly wishing the best for someone even when you know you are not the person they chose.

I don't need you to change your mind for my feelings to have been real. ❤️

✦

🌹 YOU CHANGED SOMETHING IN ME

You showed me a version of myself I didn't know existed.

A person who could care this much. A person who could wait. A person who could remember the smallest things.

A person who could keep loving… even without receiving the same love back.

"Maybe that isn't weakness. Maybe it's simply proof that my heart felt something real."
✦

🌆 EVEN THE BIGGEST CITIES…

Maybe the world will never know my side of this story. Maybe some people will call me foolish. Maybe some will call me crazy.

But I never wanted to become the world's greatest lover.

I only wanted to be someone who loved you sincerely.

And if the biggest cities in the world disappeared tomorrow, I think one memory would still remain bright in my mind.

YOU.

Your smile. Your eyes. Your presence.

"Even the most beautiful cities could fall silent and still fail to be as unforgettable as the way your eyes once made me feel."
✦

💧 IF THESE WORDS EVER BRING TEARS…

Please don't cry because you feel sorry for me.

Cry only if, somewhere inside these words, you can feel how sincerely I cared about you.

Not because I deserve you. Not because you owe me a chance. Not because you have to return my feelings.

Just this:

I LOVED YOU HONESTLY.

That's all.

✦

🎂 BUT TODAY IS YOUR DAY

Your birthday is supposed to be about you. Not my feelings. Not my sadness. Not my story.

You. ✨

So today, I don't want to ask you for anything. I don't want a promise. I don't want you to feel obligated to say anything.

I just want you to be happy.

I hope you achieve everything you secretly dream about.

I hope you meet people who appreciate your heart.

I hope you never lose that light inside you.

I hope life gives you countless reasons to smile. 🤍

✦

🌷 THE LOVE I WISH FOR YOU

If someday someone loves you the way I once wished I could love you openly…

I hope he treats your heart gently. I hope he listens when you speak. I hope he respects your dreams. I hope he makes you feel safe.

And I hope he never makes you question whether you are worthy of love.

Because you are.

✦

🌌 IN ANOTHER LIFE…

Perhaps, in another life… in another time… under different circumstances… maybe our story would have been different.

Maybe we would have met at the right time. Maybe we would have chosen each other.

But this is the life we have.

And in this life, I can only accept what is real.

So today, I will make only one wish:

Not for you to become mine.

But for you to always be happy.

Even if that happiness exists far away from me. 🤍

✦

✦ SO, HAPPY BIRTHDAY, MEHRAJ ✦

May your smile survive every difficult day.

May your dreams become bigger than your fears.

May your heart always find peace.

May every year ahead bring you closer to the life you've dreamed about.

"If I had to choose one person again, knowing everything I know now… I'd still choose her."

Maybe we'll remain a beautiful unfinished chapter. Maybe life will surprise us. Maybe it won't.

But whatever happens from here, I'll always be grateful that I got to know you.

Thank you for unknowingly becoming one of the most beautiful feelings I've ever carried.

❤️ HAPPY BIRTHDAY

To the girl I loved quietly.

To the girl I loved deeply.

To the girl I loved honestly.

And remember—

You never owed me your heart.

I simply happened to give you mine.

With all the feelings I could never put into words,

— Someone who once chose you, and still wishes the best for you. 🌙❤️
